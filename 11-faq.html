01How does the 12-hour payout guarantee work?+

The clock starts once your payout request passes verification. If funds aren't in your account within 12 hours after that, we credit you $1,000 in addition to your payout — automatically, without you needing to file a claim.

02How fast are payouts processed?+

Payout requests are processed within 24–48 hours on your scheduled payout date, straight to your linked account.

03What happens if I breach a rule?+

The account closes at the breach point — no partial credit, no extra fees. You can restart a fresh challenge anytime.

04Is my evaluation fee refundable?+

Yes. Your fee is credited back with your first successful payout on the funded account.

05Which platforms can I trade on?+

MT5, cTrader, TradeLocker, and Match-Trader are all supported, with the same rule engine enforced across every one.
