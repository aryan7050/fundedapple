Refund Policy

This page explains the current product intent around evaluation fees and refunds.

Evaluation fee

The selected challenge fee and its refund conditions are shown before checkout. A fee is not a deposit and does not represent client trading capital.

First payout

Where a plan states that the evaluation fee is refundable, the exact condition is the first successful payout on the funded account.

Before launch

This page is a product draft. Final refund terms, exclusions, timelines, and support contacts will be reviewed and published before production launch.
